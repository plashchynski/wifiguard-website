Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what the WiFi Guard iOS app (the "App") and the website www.wifiguardapp.com (the "Website") do with your information.

In short: the App does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Everything it learns about your Wi-Fi network stays on your device.

What the App does

WiFi Guard scans the Wi-Fi network you are connected to, lists the devices on it, and can notify you when a new device joins a network you chose to protect. The scan runs entirely on your device and only talks to devices on your own local network.

Information stored on your device

To remember your networks and devices between launches, the App keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • for each network you scanned: the hardware identifier (BSSID) of its router and whether you turned on protection for it;
  • for each device found on those networks: its name, any custom name you gave it, its IP address, its MAC address or other identifier, and whether it is new;
  • your App settings, such as notification preferences.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the App, and you can clear it at any time with "Reset network settings" in the App's Settings.

Permissions the App asks for

  • Local Network: lets the App find the devices on your Wi-Fi network (using ping, Bonjour, UPnP and NetBIOS requests that stay on your local network).
  • Location (While Using the App): iOS only tells an app the name and identifier of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to if the app has location permission. The App uses it for that purpose only.
  • Location (Always), optional: lets the App notice when you arrive at a place so it can re-scan a protected network in the background. Your location is not stored, sent or shared; the App only learns that you arrived somewhere.
  • Notifications: lets the App alert you about new devices. These are local notifications generated on your device.

You can change or withdraw any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Without them, parts of the App will not work.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, advertising identifier, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The App contains no third-party SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not show ads. The hardware vendor names shown for devices come from a database bundled inside the App, so no lookup is sent over the internet.

Links to other services

The App and the Website contain links to other services, such as the App Store, Google Play and our Facebook page. If you follow them, the service you open handles your information under its own privacy policy. We do not control and are not responsible for those services.

The Website

The Website is a single information page. It uses Google Analytics to count visits, which may set cookies in your browser and send your IP address and browser details to Google. You can block this with your browser's cookie settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data. The Website also loads fonts and style files from third-party content delivery networks, which see your IP address when you load the page.

Contacting us for support

If you email us, we receive the address and message you send and use them only to answer you.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
